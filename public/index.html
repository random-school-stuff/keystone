Keystone

Encrypted transport · Self-hosted

Open the web,
unlocked.

Search the web or paste any address. Keystone loads the page inside a sandboxed session so you can reach it from wherever you are. 



				

					
					
					
					
					
					
				


				

					

						
						
Encrypted in transit

						

							Your browser talks to Keystone over an encrypted Epoxy tunnel, so
							the connection stays private end to end.
						

					


					

						
						
Sandboxed session

						

							Every site runs inside an isolated service-worker scope, separate
							from your normal browsing context.
						

					


					

						
						
Yours alone

						

							Keystone runs on your own server. No third party sits between you
							and the pages you open.
